Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions.

What files will I receive?

You will receive high-resolution PNG, JPG, print-ready PDF files, and vector source files (AI/EPS) depending on your selected package.

Can manufacturers use these tech packs directly?

Yes, all tech packs are designed following garment industry standards including seam details, stitching lines, trim callouts, and measurement specs.

What if I need revisions?

Every package includes revisions. I will adjust proportions, colors, or details until you are completely satisfied with the result.

Do you accept custom package offers?

Absolutely! If you have a full collection or specific custom project, send me a message on Upwork or Fiverr for a tailored quote.
